To go live an owner completes onboarding through their secure claim/onboarding link:
1. Register with mobile number + OTP.
2. PG name, full address, Google Maps location pin.
3. PG type: male / female / co-living / other.
4. Room types, total and available beds per sharing type.
5. Monthly rent, security deposit, maintenance charges (if any).
6. Food: 2 meals / 3 meals / no food.
7. Amenities and house rules.
8. At least 5 clear photos (room, bathroom, common area, entrance, kitchen/dining).
9. Contact details and required owner documents, uploaded only through the secure upload — never over WhatsApp or a call.
10. Review and submit. The NiwasNest team verifies; the listing goes live only after approval, or the owner is asked for corrections.
Never ask for Aadhaar numbers, OTPs or bank passwords in chat or calls.
