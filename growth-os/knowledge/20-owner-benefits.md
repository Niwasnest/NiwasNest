Listing a PG on NiwasNest is free. Owners get:
- Online visibility to students and working professionals searching by area, map, budget and sharing type.
- A digital listing with photos, room types (single/double/multiple sharing), rent, deposit, food, amenities and house rules.
- Enquiries and booking requests from tenants, with an owner dashboard to manage rooms, beds, vacancies, tenants and rent.
- Vacancy updates so only available beds are shown.
- Optional promotions and referral opportunities.
NiwasNest does not guarantee bookings or occupancy. It is an additional channel alongside brokers, boards and word of mouth.
Commission or fee details: never state from memory. Always use the get_commercial_policy tool.
