Every campaign must have: name, audience, start and end date, eligibility, reward/discount (if any), budget, maximum redemptions, tracking code / unique QR, terms, and approval status.
Success metrics: verified live PGs and completed bookings (check-ins). Cost per live PG and cost per completed booking decide whether a campaign scales. Calls, messages, scans or registrations alone are not success.
Run every new idea as a small pilot with a measurable target first. No advertising money is approved unless the founder approves the specific spend.
