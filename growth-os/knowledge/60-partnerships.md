Offer: help incoming students, trainees and new joiners find verified PG accommodation near campus or office, with transparent rent and zero brokerage.
Partnership formats: accommodation help page or QR desk for orientation/joining; student or campus ambassador referral program; placement/joining accommodation assistance; event participation.
Email principles: one institution at a time, mention the specific campus and nearby localities, address the right office (placement, admissions, hostel, HR), keep under 150 words, one clear ask (a 15-minute call), include an opt-out line. Never claim an existing partnership, student numbers or results we do not have.
