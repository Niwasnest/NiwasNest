On niwasnest.com tenants can search PGs by city and area, use the map, filter by gents/ladies/co-living and budget, compare sharing types, rent, deposit, food and amenities, view photos, save PGs and send an enquiry or book.
Zero brokerage: tenants pay only the charges shown on the property page.
Search: https://niwasnest.com/search
Owners: list your PG from the website (List your PG).
Referral: customers can share a PG link and earn a reward when the friend books and checks in, per the referral terms shown in the app.
