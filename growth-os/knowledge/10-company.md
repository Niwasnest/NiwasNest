NiwasNest Technologies Private Limited runs NiwasNest (https://niwasnest.com), a PG, hostel and co-living discovery and booking marketplace. Tagline: "Your Home Away From Home".
It connects PG owners and hostel operators who have vacant beds with students, freshers, interns and working professionals looking for accommodation.
Focus market: Bengaluru (Electronic City, HSR Layout, BTM Layout, Silk Board, Marathahalli, Whitefield, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road, Koramangala, Bommanahalli, Kudlu Gate, Bommasandra, Hebbal, Yelahanka). The website also lists PGs in Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, Chennai and Delhi NCR.
For tenants NiwasNest is zero-brokerage: tenants pay only the charges shown on the property page.
Listings are verified by the NiwasNest team before they go live.
